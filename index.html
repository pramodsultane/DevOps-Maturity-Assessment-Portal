Scoring & Interpretation Guide
// Table 1
Scoring Model

Each control is scored on a 0 to 5 scale. The table below defines each maturity level:

Score Level Description Evidence Required
0 Not Implemented No activity exists in this area. No awareness or tooling present. None – control does not exist
1 Initial / Ad-hoc Some isolated efforts exist, but they are undocumented and inconsistent across teams. Informal evidence, verbal confirmation only
2 Partially Implemented Control is implemented for some projects or teams but not consistently enforced organization wide. Partial documentation, some projects compliant
3 Defined & Documented Control is formally documented, adopted by most teams, and regularly reviewed. Policy documentation, team adoption evidence
4 Fully Implemented & Enforced Control is fully implemented, enforced via automation (CI/CD, policy gates), and monitored. Automated enforcement, audit logs, dashboards
5 Optimized & Continuously Improved Control is mature with continuous improvement cycles, feedback loops, and benchmarking. KPIs, trend analysis, improvement records
// Table 2
Score Interpretation

Total scores are calculated by summing scores across all controls. Use the following table to interpret overall organizational maturity:

Overall Score Maturity Level Interpretation Recommended Actions
0 – 20 🔴 Beginner Critical gaps exist. DevOps practices are largely absent or ad-hoc. Immediate remediation required. Prioritize foundational controls.
20 – 45 🟠 Developing Some practices are in place, but significant inconsistencies remain across teams. Focus on standardization and documentation of existing practices.
45 – 70 🟣 Maturing DevOps practices are defined and mostly implemented. Some automation gaps exist. Enforce automation and broaden adoption across all teams.
70 – 90 🔵 Advanced Strong DevOps culture with broad automation, enforcement, and visibility. Focus on optimization, continuous improvement, and benchmarking.
90 – 110 🟢 Optimized World-class DevOps practices with continuous improvement and full observability. Sustain excellence and drive industry-leading innovations.
